About Andrew

The lawyer they called when the rulebook did not exist yet.

Within a decade of admission, in front of the Supreme Court.

Most attorneys spend a career applying law that is already written. Andrew Cooper made his name on the harder problem: what happens when the technology arrives before the regulation does.

He began at Shook, Hardy & Bacon LLP as a patent litigator, moving quickly across the full range of federal practice — including filing as amicus curiae to the Supreme Court of the United States on esoteric patent matters. Within ten years of starting his career he had risen to Vice President and Head of Legal for UPS Airlines, and became the first Black General Counsel in that airline’s history.

He credits his parents, Beatrice and Andrew Lee Cooper, and his mentors, Dinisa Folmar and Leonard Searcy — a list he offers before he offers his own accomplishments.
