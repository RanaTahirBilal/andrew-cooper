About

The lawyer they called when the rulebook did not exist yet

Most attorneys spend a career applying law that is already written. Andrew Cooper made his name on the harder problem: what happens when the technology arrives before the regulation does.

He began at Shook, Hardy & Bacon LLP, the international firm where he worked as a patent litigator and moved quickly across the full range of federal practice — including filing as amicus curiae to the Supreme Court of the United States on esoteric patent matters. Within a decade of beginning his professional career, he had risen to Vice President and Head of Legal for UPS Airlines.

Securing the full certification under Federal Aviation Administration regulations for the world’s first drone-based airline for United Parcel Service. Andrew Cooper, on his most notable accomplishment

That certification was not a filing exercise. It meant persuading a federal regulator to license a category of operation that had never been licensed anywhere — and doing it ahead of the largest technology companies in the world, who were pursuing the same approval. In 2022 he moved to Meta, where his portfolio spans strategic transactions and M&A, intellectual property licensing and open source.

He credits his parents, Beatrice and Andrew Lee Cooper, and his mentors, Dinisa Folmar and Leonard Searcy — a list he offers before he offers his own accomplishments.

  2. Through 2022

    Led the legal function for one of the largest cargo airlines in the world, and the first Black General Counsel of UPS Airlines. The signature achievement: full FAA certification for the world’s first fully unmanned drone airline — an approval no other operator, including the largest technology companies, had obtained.
